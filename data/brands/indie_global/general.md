# Indie Global Publications Overview

Indie Global Publications provides world-class editorial, design, distribution, and marketing services for independent authors. We help authors transform their manuscripts into timeless artifacts. Our goal is to address the multi-faceted reasons that block aspiring authors from actualizing their dreams. We connect your manuscript to a network of 40,000+ retail partners across 150 countries.

## The Publishing Journey
From initial concept to global distribution, we demystify every step of the process.

1. **Submit**: Send us your manuscript. Our experts provide an initial assessment and roadmap.
2. **Refine**: Professional editing, formatting, and custom cover design to make your book shine.
3. **Publish**: We handle the technical setup for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and more.
4. **Launch**: Your book is live! We help you reach global audiences with strategic marketing.

## FAQ
**Q: How long does the process take?**
A: The timeline varies based on the services required, but most manuscripts move from curation to global distribution within 4–6 months. This ensures we maintain the highest editorial and design standards.

**Q: Do I keep my rights?**
A: Absolutely. You retain 100% of the rights to your intellectual property. We are a service provider, not a traditional publisher that takes your rights.

**Q: Where will my book be distributed?**
A: We distribute to over 40,000 retail partners worldwide, including Amazon, IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble, and independent bookstores across 150 countries.

**Q: Do you help with marketing?**
A: While our core focus is on production and distribution, we provide strategic guidance on author branding and can connect you with specialized marketing partners.
