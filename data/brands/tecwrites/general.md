# TecWrites Overview

TecWrites is a hybrid technology and writing agency specializing in high-impact technical writing, content strategy, web development, SEO, and digital marketing.

Website: https://www.tecwrites.com/

## Core Services
1. **Technical Writing & Documentation**:
   - API documentation, developer guides, whitepapers, and technical manuals.
   - Comprehensive content strategy, ghostwriting, and editorial reviews.
2. **Web & App Development**:
   - Full-stack web application development, custom landing pages, and responsive UI design.
   - Frontend and backend engineering with modern frameworks.
3. **SEO & Growth Strategy**:
   - Technical SEO audits, keyword research, on-page and off-page optimization.
   - Content marketing strategies engineered to drive organic search rankings.
4. **Branding & Visual Identity**:
   - Brand guidelines, logo design, typography, and marketing assets.
