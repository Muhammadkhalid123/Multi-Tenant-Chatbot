# Kandle Direct Publishing Overview

Kandle Direct Publishing (KDP Partner Services) is a premier author-services platform providing comprehensive publishing, distribution, and promotional solutions for independent authors and publishers worldwide.

Website: https://www.kandledirectpublishing.com/

## Core Services
1. **Manuscript Preparation & Editorial**:
   - Comprehensive Developmental Editing, Copyediting, and Proofreading.
   - Professional Manuscript Assessment and Critical Reader Reviews.
2. **Book Design & Formatting**:
   - Custom High-Converting Book Cover Design (eBook, Paperback, Hardcover).
   - Interior Typesetting and Formatting for Amazon Kindle, EPUB, and Print-on-Demand (POD).
3. **Global Distribution**:
   - Worldwide distribution across Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP), IngramSpark, Barnes & Noble Press, Apple Books, Google Play Books, and Kobo.
   - Reaching over 40,000 retailers, libraries, and universities globally.
4. **ISBN & Rights Management**:
   - Official ISBN assignment and barcode generation.
   - Copyright registration and metadata optimization.
   - Authors retain 100% intellectual property rights and 100% net royalties.
5. **Author Marketing & Promotion**:
   - Amazon Bestseller Launch Campaigns and Amazon Ads management.
   - Author Website Design, SEO optimization, and Book Promotion Trailers.

## FAQ
